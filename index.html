    How MineSafe AI Works

    The system ingests geological, environmental, and operational data from mine sites, processes it through a feature engineering pipeline, and feeds it into ensemble machine learning models trained on real-world datasets. A composite risk score is generated in real-time, alongside actionable safety recommendations.

    In parallel, our YOLO-based computer vision module processes slope surveillance video to detect rockfall events as they happen, enabling rapid alert and response.
